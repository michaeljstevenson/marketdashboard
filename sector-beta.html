Methodology

A different lens from this site's other sector pages. Sector Correlation Matrix asks how strongly sectors move together. Sector Rotation Model asks which sectors are leading or lagging on price momentum. Neither asks how much a sector amplifies the market's own moves, which is what beta measures — a sector can be highly correlated with SPY and still have a beta well above or below 1.0, and a sector's beta can drift over time even when its correlation to the market stays roughly the same.

Rolling beta is the slope of an ordinary least-squares regression of a sector ETF's daily returns on SPY's daily returns, over the trailing 63 trading sessions (one calendar quarter), recomputed for every trading day in the lookback window. Beta dispersion is the spread between the highest and lowest of the 11 sectors' rolling betas on a given day. Beta range (in the tables) is each sector's own max minus min rolling beta over the whole window — how much its risk profile itself has moved, not how far from 1.0 it sits.

Data. Reuses the same ~2 years of daily adjusted-close history already fetched for Sector Performance (the 11 SPDR sector ETFs plus SPY) rather than pulling any new data — pure client-side analysis of an existing dataset, the same approach Sector Correlation Matrix and Sector Rotation Model already use. The trailing 3-month relative return in the table comes from that same payload's own precomputed figure.

Test. The scatter pools one point per sector per non-overlapping quarter (roughly 6-7 quarters × 11 sectors) — a sector's rolling beta at the start of the quarter against its actual relative return over the quarter that followed — Pearson (OLS) and Spearman rank correlation, same convention as Factor Analysis. Non-overlapping quarters avoid the mechanical correlation two heavily-overlapping rolling windows would otherwise share, the same overlapping-window pitfall Share Count Trends' own methodology flags; because the same 11 sectors reappear across quarters, this is still a repeated-sector panel, not a fully independent sample, and is reported as descriptive evidence rather than a strong significance claim on this small a universe.

Source: Alpha Vantage/Yahoo Finance daily price history for the 11 SPDR sector ETFs and SPY, via Sector Performance's own daily job
