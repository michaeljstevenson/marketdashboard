Methodology

This page makes no Alpha Vantage calls of its own — it joins three snapshots this site already publishes: ROIC vs. Cost of Capital for each company's return on invested capital, Equity Risk Premium by Sector for each company's trailing earnings yield, and Relative Strength Leaders/Laggards for the 3-month relative-return test. Universe is every symbol with a usable figure on both of the first two pages (a company missing either one can't be ranked, since the Magic Formula needs both legs).

This is a deliberate adaptation of Greenblatt's original formula, not an exact replication, and the deviations are worth being explicit about: Greenblatt's own "return on capital" is EBIT ÷ (net working capital + net fixed assets), and his "earnings yield" is EBIT ÷ enterprise value — both built to be capital-structure-neutral (pre-interest, pre-tax-shield). This page substitutes this site's own already-computed ROIC (NOPAT ÷ invested capital) and trailing-P/E-based earnings yield (100 ÷ P/E) as readily-available stand-ins, reusing two pages that already exist rather than standing up a third full-universe statement sweep for figures that would tell a very similar story. Each company is ranked twice — once by ROIC (highest first) and once by earnings yield (highest first) — and the two ranks are simply added together; the combined rank sorts ascending, so the single best possible outcome is rank 1 + rank 1 = 2. Ties are broken by ROIC.

The regression test is a single-snapshot cross-section (no accumulated history needed): combined rank vs. 3-month relative return asks whether the market currently rewards this screen's picks with better relative price performance — not whether the screen predicts future returns, which is the actual historical claim behind the strategy and isn't something a single snapshot can test. Uses the same Pearson-and-Spearman two-method check as Factor Analysis.

No schedule of its own to run or maintain — this page simply reads whatever ROIC vs. Cost of Capital and Equity Risk Premium by Sector's own snapshots currently say, live, on every page load, so it's automatically as fresh as the more recently-updated of those two.

Source: this site's own ROIC vs. Cost of Capital, Equity Risk Premium by Sector, and Relative Strength Leaders/Laggards data, all ultimately sourced from Alpha Vantage
