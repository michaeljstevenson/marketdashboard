Reading this chart

Each bubble is one company (Daily) or one SPDR Select Sector ETF (Annual). Horizontal position is the company’s GICS sector; vertical position is its return — close-to-close for the session in Daily, calendar-year total return in Annual. Bubble area is market capitalization (Daily) or the sector’s approximate weight in the index (Annual). The dashed line marks SPY’s return over the same window.

Method & caveats

Daily snapshots are captured after each close and archived going forward; there is no historical backfill. Returns come from Alpha Vantage delayed quotes; market caps are shares outstanding × last price, with sector and share counts refreshed weekly. Index membership is the current S&P 500 constituent list applied to every archived day — a company added or removed later is not reconstructed point-in-time.

Annual uses the 11 SPDR Select Sector ETFs (total return, split & dividend adjusted) for calendar years over roughly the last 15 years. XLRE (Real Estate) begins in 2016 and XLC (Communication Services) in 2019 — earlier years omit those bubbles. Sector weights used for bubble size are approximate year-end figures.

Source: Alpha Vantage — SPDR Select Sector ETFs, SPY, and S&P 500 constituent quotes. Visualization after Jan at Chartfleau.
