IV. Reads

01

The index level is the least representative number on the page.

A 1.4% drawdown at the index coexists with a 24.9% median drawdown at the member level. Equal-weighting closes part of that gap (the equal-weighted index, RSP, is 5.6% below ATH). Cap-weighting is doing the rest.

index −1.4% · equal-weight −5.6% · median member −24.9% · mean member −30.1%
02

Nothing is at an all-time high.

The closest company in the S&P 500 is just 0.5% below its ATH, and only 19 members are within 5%. The S&P 500 index being within 1.4% of its record is being driven almost entirely by its largest companies.

within 5% of ATH: 19 (3.78%) · best member −0.5%
03

The distribution has a heavy tail.

All-time high vs. 52-week high: 87 members are more than 50% below their ATH and 28 are more than 70% below, against 16 and zero respectively on a 52-week basis.

50%+ below: 87 vs 16 · 60%+: 53 vs 4 · 70%+: 28 vs 0
04

Real estate is the cleanest illustration of the two lenses.

On a 52-week basis, real estate is among the most resilient sectors at 27% of members in drawdown. On an all-time high basis, it is the worst in the index at 80%, with a median member 36% below its record. The sector has been stable for a year and has not recovered from what came before.

Real Estate: ATH basis 24/30 (80%), median −36% · 52wk basis 8/30 (27%)
05

Technology is worse than the index on both measures.

56 of 87 technology members are more than 20% below their ATH, the sector median is −30%, worse than the index median. The sector whose largest constituents are holding the index near its record contains one of the weaker member distributions in it.

Technology: 56/87 (64%) vs index 60% · median −30% vs index −25%
06

Financials are the only sector where most members are intact.

At 41% of members in drawdown and a median of −17%, financials are the sole sector below the index rate on both lenses. Basic Materials and Industrials follow. The spread between the best and worst sector is 39 points on the ATH basis.

Financials 29/70 (41%), median −17% · worst–best spread 39pp
07

Concentration is back near its four-year extreme.

The cap-weighted/equal-weighted ratio has retraced to 3.65, against a peak of 3.68 in May 2026 and a low of 2.71 in January 2023.

ratio 3.65 · peak 3.68 (May 2026) · low 2.71 (Jan 2023) · YTD cap-wt +12.5% vs equal-wt +9.8%
