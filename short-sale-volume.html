Methodology

Short interest is the number of shares held short on a settlement date, as brokers report it to FINRA twice a month. Here it's shown as a share of each company's shares outstanding (from Alpha Vantage's company overview) and as days to cover, FINRA's ratio of short interest to average daily volume. FINRA's free files include exchange-listed stocks from 06/15/2021.

The forward-return test uses every settlement date since 10/29/2021. At each one, the stocks then in the S&P 500 are split into tenths by days to cover, and each tenth's average return is compared with the S&P 500 total return index over the next 1, 3 and 6 months. Returns start 10 trading days after the settlement date, once FINRA has published it, so the test only uses figures that were public at the time. Results are averaged across settlement dates, with Newey-West standard errors because neighboring windows overlap. Companies that no longer trade are left out.

Short sale volume is a different measure: the share of a stock's daily trading volume that was executed as a short sale. A high short sale volume ratio doesn't necessarily mean a big short position is building, since some of that volume is closed out the same day or is market makers hedging. It's an imperfect stand-in for short-selling activity.

The short sale volume ratio (SVR) = short volume ÷ total volume for each day. The sector chart, leaderboards and table use each stock's average SVR over the last ~21 trading days. The trend chart plots the market-wide median SVR each day.

The forward-return test splits the ~3-month window into two halves that don't overlap. Each stock's average SVR in the first half is compared with its return relative to the S&P 500 in the second half. A negative relationship would match research finding that short sellers are informed (e.g. Diether, Lee & Werner, 2009): more shorting before weaker returns. A flat or positive one means that isn't showing up here. Pearson (linear) and Spearman (rank) fits are both shown.

Source: FINRA equity short interest files and FINRA daily short sale volume files, shares outstanding from Alpha Vantage, prices from Yahoo Finance.
