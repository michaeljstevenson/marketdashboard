Streak Scanner

Every streak condition the odd-stats engine tracks — the S&P 500 back to 1927, each of the 11 SPDR sector ETFs, and every pair among the index, the sectors, and its ten largest companies (individually and as a basket) running in step, against each other, or closing their gap. Live streaks sit up top with the full history of every prior time they reached this length and what markets did over the following year; the rest show how far along they are and how past occurrences played out. Counts update as streaks extend.

Live conditions are analysed at their current run length; the number and its history update as the streak extends. Dormant conditions (the ones still short of their minimum length) show what happened the last times they did reach it. Rarity, for a live streak, is the share of this condition's past qualifying runs that never got this long — near 0% for a fresh streak, climbing as it extends. Forward-return columns summarise the analog set: prior runs that reached the same length, de-duplicated so one long streak counts once, tracked over the next year (samples shrink at the longer horizons as recent analogs run out of runway). Regenerate with node scripts/oddstats-detect.js --dump. Food for thought, not a signal.
