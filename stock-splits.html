Methodology

Split events come from Alpha Vantage's SPLITS endpoint, swept across the full S&P 500 and kept for the trailing six years. Each event records an effective date and a split factor (2.0 for a 2-for-1 split, 0.1 for a 1-for-10 reverse split); factors above 1 are classified as forward splits, factors below 1 as reverse splits. Company name and sector are reused from the Sector Beeswarm page's own weekly metadata sweep rather than a second full-universe call.

The performance test is built only on forward splits at least 370 days old (long enough for a genuine 1-year forward return to exist), using TIME_SERIES_DAILY_ADJUSTED total-return price history for the split company and for SPY over the identical window. Excess return is the stock's total return minus SPY's total return, both measured from the first trading day on or after the split's effective date — this isolates whatever the market did with the specific stock from what the broader market did over the same stretch, rather than crediting a split with returns that were really just a bull market. Reverse splits are excluded from this test: they're a different, largely distress-driven signal (often used to avoid a delisting-minimum-price rule) rather than the growth signal a forward split typically represents, so pooling the two would muddy the result.

The chart above the ratio distribution reports the average excess return at each horizon alongside a one-sample t-test against zero — using the same normal-approximation p-value construction as the regressions on Factor Analysis and this site's other pages, applied here to "is the mean excess return different from zero" rather than to a slope. A positive, statistically significant result would support the classic Ikenberry-style stock-split anomaly finding; a small or insignificant one would suggest that, at least in this sample, the market already prices in whatever information a split conveys.

Refreshes weekly (Saturday, after the market close) — split events are sparse enough that a daily re-sweep would mostly refetch unchanged history.

Source: Alpha Vantage — SPLITS, TIME_SERIES_DAILY_ADJUSTED
